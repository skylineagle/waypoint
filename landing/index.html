Itinerary

Your whole trip.
One day at a time.

The itinerary opens on today. Stops in order, how to get between them, the booking coming up and tonight’s bed. Every other day is a swipe away.

Up next, up top.

The next stop leads the day, notes and all. One tap for directions in Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze. One tap to mark it done.

The walk in between.

Every leg says how you’ll get there. A 9 minute walk. An 18 minute train. So you know when to leave before you have to.

Every stop, on a map.

Open the map and the day’s pins are numbered in the order you’ll walk them, with the route drawn between. Weather for the next stop sits one tap away.

Tomorrow, already packed.

Swipe to any day. Travel days line up the train, the check-in and tonight’s hotel, with the 10:00 Romancecar already booked.

City to city.

When the day covers a country, the map does too. Shinjuku to the hot springs of Hakone, 89 km, in one line.

Built for TREK.

Waypoint is the iPhone companion to TREK, the open‑source trip planner you host yourself. Plan the trip on the web with the people you travel with. Then put it in your pocket.

  1. 1

    Run TREK.

    TREK runs on a server you choose: a home lab, a NAS or a small VPS. Your trips live there, and only there.

  2. 2
    trek.yourdomain.com

    Point Waypoint at it.

    Type your server’s address and sign in with your TREK account. That’s the whole setup.

  3. 3

    Go.

    Stops, bookings, budget and to-dos are already there. Expenses and to-dos you add on the road are saved to TREK.

Waypoint needs a TREK server.

It doesn’t host one for you and has no account of its own. If you already use TREK, you’re set. If not, TREK is free and open source.

Get TREK on GitHub

Your trip stays between
you and your server.

Waypoint talks to the TREK server you typed in, and that’s it. The person who made the app can’t see your trips, your spending or where you are.

  • No account with us.

    You sign in to your own TREK server. The sign-in lives in your iPhone’s Keychain.

  • No analytics. No ads.

    Nothing is tracked, nothing is sold, nothing phones home.

  • Location stays local.

    Your location is used on the device to notice arrivals. It’s never sent to the developer.

  • Receipts read on device.

    Merchant, total and category are worked out on your iPhone.1

  1. Receipt scanning and on-device categorisation use Apple Intelligence and need a supported iPhone. On other iPhones you add expenses by hand, and categories come from keywords.

  3. Marking stops done automatically uses your location, including while the app is closed. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.
  4. Exchange rates come from Frankfurter. Only a currency code is sent.
  5. Screens shown are from a real trip planned in TREK, simulated on iPhone.
