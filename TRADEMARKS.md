# Waypoint trademark policy

The Waypoint name, app icon, and logos identify the official project and releases maintained by Ofek Nesher. This policy governs use of those marks.

Waypoint's AGPL-3.0 code license does not grant rights under trademark law to use these marks. This policy concerns branding only; it does not restrict the rights to use, modify, or redistribute code granted by the AGPL.

## Permitted use

- Clone, modify, and build Waypoint for private use while retaining its existing branding.
- Keep the original name, logos, and notices in source forks, provided the fork is clearly identified as unofficial or modified and does not claim endorsement.
- Use the Waypoint name in truthful references, attribution, compatibility statements, reviews, documentation, and links to the official project.
- Show the official app's logo or screenshots when discussing or documenting Waypoint, without suggesting sponsorship or endorsement.

## Public builds and services

When publishing an independently built app, a modified app, or a service based on Waypoint, use a distinct product name, app icon, and logos unless you have prior written permission from Ofek Nesher. This applies to free and paid releases, including app-store listings.

You may describe such a product as "based on Waypoint" or "compatible with Waypoint" when accurate. Do not present it as an official Waypoint release, or suggest that Ofek Nesher maintains, sponsors, or endorses it.

Do not use the Waypoint marks or confusingly similar branding as your own product name, company name, domain name, or trademark. Required copyright notices and truthful attribution must remain intact when rebranding.

## Permission and other marks

Request permission through the [Waypoint repository](https://github.com/skylineagle/waypoint/issues). Permission is granted only by an explicit written response from Ofek Nesher. Uses permitted by applicable law do not require permission under this policy.
