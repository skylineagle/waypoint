Costs · Trek style

Look follows TREK's mobile Costs tab: the dark #15151A hero, bordered 16px cards, uppercase eyebrows, and TREK's 14 fixed category colors and icons. UX comes from AppLlama: TravelSpend (day-grouped ledger, add sheet tinted by category with big amount, currency chip and category grid), Spendee (donut plus category %), Easy Calc (glanceable converter).
Totals are shown in your TREK default_currency (ILS) and converted from the trip currency (JPY) with the same Frankfurter rates TREK uses. The real app draws the charts with Swift Charts.
