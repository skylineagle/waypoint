Trek Companion — onboarding variants

Based on AppLlama references: Tripsy (travel planner welcome: warm gradient, photo cards, app icon, orange pill CTA), Alfred (one question per screen + segmented progress), Splitwise (clear labelled fields, password eye). In every variant the server is checked before you sign in (Trek's public /api/auth/app-config), so a typo in the URL shows up immediately instead of as a failed login.

A · Warm welcome + sign-in sheet

A Tripsy-style welcome with travel cards and the app icon. "Get Started" slides up a sheet where you enter the server, then email and password. Friendly and on-brand for a travel app, with two screens in total.
